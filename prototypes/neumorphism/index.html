Questions

Does Calma work with my heating?

Calma works with most radiator and floor heating systems, and with heat pumps that accept on/off or OpenTherm control. The compatibility check in the app takes two minutes.

Do I need an installer?

Most homes install Calma One themselves in about 20 minutes. If your current thermostat runs on 230 V, ask an electrician.

What happens if the Wi-Fi is down?

The dial and room sensors keep working on their own Thread network. Schedules run on the device.

Can I use Calma without the app?

Yes. The dial sets the temperature for the whole home. The app adds rooms, schedules and the energy report.

Where is my data stored?

Readings stay on the device. Only the monthly energy report is stored in Switzerland, and you can turn it off.
