Straight answers.

Anything else? Ask the crew at crew@nightshift.example. A person replies within a day.

Do I need a team?

No. Come alone. Friday's team matching puts you with people who need your skills.

Who owns what we build?

Your team does. We only ask to show your demo video on our site.

Is it really free for students?

Yes, with a student email. Food, power and mentors are included.

Can I sleep there?

Yes. Book a bunk in the quiet room, which stays dark and phone-free all weekend.

What should I bring?

A laptop, its charger, a refillable bottle, and an idea if you have one.
