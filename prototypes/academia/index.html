Method and transparency

Evidence should be inspectable.

Every project is designed to make its evidence, analytical choices, and limits legible. We publish enough context for readers to understand what a finding can—and cannot—support.

Open by default Methods and limitations travel with every publication.

Traceable source records

Datasets, interviews, observations, and exclusions are documented by source and date.

Visible analytical choices

Definitions, transformations, assumptions, and revisions travel with each output.

Limitations in plain language

Uncertainty and missing evidence are presented with findings, not hidden in an appendix.

Versioned public outputs

Corrections and substantive changes are recorded so prior interpretations remain understandable.
