System

Every Raster unit is built from three parts. Each part is made in Biel and sold separately.

  • N-20NodeSteel cube, 20 mm. Joins up to six tubes.
  • T-250
    T-500    TubeSquare steel tube, 20 × 20 mm, in 250 and 500 mm lengths.
  • P-500PanelSheet steel, 1.2 mm, powder-coated. Used for shelves, backs, sides and doors.

Configurations
