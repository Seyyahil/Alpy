Modular engineering

Open by design.

No sealed frame electronics. No proprietary mystery box. The parts most likely to wear or evolve are designed to leave the bicycle with standard workshop tools.

01
Battery cartridgeSlides out in seconds; housing, cells and controller are serviced as separate parts.
02
Drive moduleFour accessible fasteners release the motor without opening the bottom bracket.
03
Control layerDisplay, lights and wiring loom disconnect through keyed, weather-sealed plugs.
04
Carry systemOne frame interface accepts racks, child seats, baskets and cargo modules.

Urban ride story

The city is the product test.

Wet rails. Short lights. Two bridges and one loaded rack. Shift One is tuned for the uneven rhythm of ordinary urban travel, not a laboratory range loop.

Assistance arrives smoothly below 25 km/h, then disappears without drag when the street opens.

Repair and service

The repair manual is part of the bicycle.

Exploded diagrams, part numbers and service intervals are public. Owners can work locally, visit a Shift partner, or send a module—not a complete bicycle.
