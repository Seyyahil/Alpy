The hotel

Architecture with a quiet pulse.

Art Deco Hotel restores the movement’s metropolitan confidence through stepped architecture, cream-and-black stone, lacquered walnut, fluted glass, and sculptural brasswork.

From the tiered lobby lights to the stillness of each guest room, every detail carries the same precise geometry and an assured, unhurried welcome.
