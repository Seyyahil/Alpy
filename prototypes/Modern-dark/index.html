Resources / FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

The essentials before you start your first plan.

What can I plan in Keeplane?

You can organize projects with tasks, project boards, deadlines, team updates, and shared files.

Does Keeplane support team workflows?

Yes. Shared boards and team updates help everyone work from the same current project view.

Can I keep files with the project?

Yes. File sharing keeps important project material close to the tasks, decisions, and updates it supports.

Does Keeplane work with other tools?

Keeplane is designed to connect with the surrounding workflow. Specific connector availability depends on your setup.

Can I start without a paid plan?

Yes. You can start planning free and move to a team workflow when your project needs it.
